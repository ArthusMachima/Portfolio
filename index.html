Pawn Turn Crisis

A turn-based combat x shooter game where the player needs to strategize how to survive while fending off from instrusive chess pieces and more. I made this game for my school organization's little gamejam.

Father's Mansion

Father's Mansion is a puzzle escape game with a narrative being told in a perspective of a blue-haired girl with a rugged brown jacket who seems to not have an idea why they ended up there. Collect items and clues scattered throughout a mysterious mansion and the blue-haired girl may find a way to escape. This game was made for a group project in one of my majors.

Rush in Time City (Prototype)

A prototype of a platformer game where you rush through the corridors dodging and parrying enemy bullets as you reach the end of the corridors to stop a catastrophe on a time limit. The game was made in two days as an activity for a major subject.

DE:BUG

DE:BUG is a game about shooting literal bugs. The game was my first attempt on implementing proceedural map generation and inventory system.
